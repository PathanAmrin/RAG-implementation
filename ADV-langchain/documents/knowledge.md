Sample Company Policy Manual — RAG Demonstration Document
Document ID: HR-POL-001    Version: 2.0    Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Purpose: This fictional policy document is designed as sample source material for a Retrieval-Augmented
Generation (RAG) application. It contains realistic policy information that can be chunked, embedded, stored in
a vector database, retrieved, and used by an LLM to answer employee questions.
1. Working Hours and Attendance
Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Employees are expected to
complete their assigned working hours and maintain regular attendance.
• Employees should record attendance through the company attendance system.
• A late arrival of more than 30 minutes should be communicated to the reporting manager.
• Employees working remotely must remain available during core hours from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
• Repeated attendance violations may result in corrective action according to HR procedures.
2. Leave Policy
Employees may request leave through the HR portal. Leave approval depends on business requirements,
available leave balance, and manager approval.
• Casual Leave: 12 days per calendar year.
• Sick Leave: 10 days per calendar year.
• Earned/Privilege Leave: 15 days per calendar year.
• Planned leave should normally be requested at least 3 working days in advance.
• Emergency leave should be communicated to the reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible.
3. Work From Home Policy
Eligible employees may work from home with prior manager approval. Employees must maintain a professional
working environment and remain reachable during core working hours.
Company equipment must be used primarily for authorized business activities. Confidential company
information must not be stored on personal devices unless explicitly approved.
4. Information Security
Employees are responsible for protecting company information, credentials, customer data, and intellectual
property.
• Passwords must not be shared with colleagues or external persons.
• Multi-factor authentication must be enabled for supported company systems.
• Confidential files must be stored only in approved company systems.
• Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT Security Team immediately.
• Employees must not install unauthorized software on company-managed devices.
5. Acceptable Use of Company Systems
Company laptops, email accounts, cloud services, and network resources are provided for legitimate business
purposes. Limited personal use may be permitted when it does not interfere with work, violate company policy,
or create a security risk.
Employees must not use company systems to distribute malicious software, conduct unauthorized access, or
transmit confidential information to unauthorized recipients.
6. Employee Conduct
Employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully toward colleagues, customers, vendors, and
business partners.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, workplace violence, and intentional misconduct are prohibited. Concerns
may be reported to HR or through the company's confidential reporting channel.
7. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses may be reimbursed when they are reasonable, necessary, and supported by appropriate
documentation.
• Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
• Receipts are required for individual expenses above n1,000.
• Travel expenses must comply with the company's approved travel guidelines.
• Manager approval is required before reimbursement is processed.
8. Performance Reviews
Employees generally participate in a formal performance review twice each year. Reviews may consider
delivery of goals, quality of work, collaboration, communication, customer impact, and professional
development.
9. Training and Learning
Employees may participate in company-sponsored training programs based on role requirements and available
budgets. Employees are encouraged to maintain relevant technical and professional skills.
10. Data Privacy
Personal and customer information must be collected, accessed, processed, and shared only for legitimate
business purposes and in accordance with applicable laws and company procedures.
Employees must report suspected unauthorized disclosure or loss of personal information to the appropriate
security or privacy contact.
11. Policy Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy require written approval from the appropriate department head or HR representative.
An exception may be temporary and may include specific conditions.
12. Policy Questions
Employees with questions about this policy should contact the Human Resources department. The latest
approved version of company policies should always be treated as authoritative.
